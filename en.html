Orders stuck on “Waiting For Agg”: DoorDash held back its webhooks for about 3 hours

Root cause (high confidence): upstream at DoorDash. From 00:10 to 03:10 UTC (03:10–06:10 Israel) DoorDash created every delivery instantly but delivered its status webhooks 20–41 minutes late. Stores never learned a courier was assigned, so orders sat on “Waiting For Agg” while drivers were already at the counter. At 03:00 UTC DoorDash released the backlog and everything recovered on its own.

On our side, every update went from our service, through the queues, to the store app within one second. Nothing on our side changed, failed or was deployed.

Unusual hours

  • 00:00 UTC (03:00 IL), onset: all webhooks still look normal, but status updates dropped to 5.7 per order (normally 8.6–9.4). 3,240 orders waited over 10 minutes.
  • 01:00–02:59 UTC (04:00–05:59 IL), the core: all webhooks fell 48% and 60% vs the previous night, even though there were more orders. 15–18 webhooks per order instead of about 40. Canada fell 51% and 67%.
  • 03:00 UTC (06:00 IL), the release: webhooks up 26% and status updates up 71% (26,572 vs 15,515). 14.2 status updates per order, the highest of all three nights. Stores got the whole backlog at once.
  • Status updates vs all webhooks: at 01–02 the status updates still arrived at about the usual hourly count, just 25–30 minutes late. Most of the drop in all webhooks is driver location pings (every 30 seconds), which DoorDash apparently never sent.
  • Previous night, 00:00 UTC: same pattern on a smaller scale. Median 8:56, 2,949 orders over 10 minutes, 29.9 webhooks per order (the lowest that night). Started at 23:40.
  • Oct 14 19:00 UTC (22:00 IL): another small event. 83 orders over 10 minutes, 90th percentile 138 seconds, and status updates dipped to 1,679 in the 19:10–19:20 window. The same hour on Oct 15 was clean (median 19 seconds, only 2 orders).

Does Canada get 4 times more callbacks?

No. The ×4 is the ratio between all webhooks and the status updates saved in audit, and it exists in both regions. Audit saves a row only when the status changes; it does not save the driver location pings (every 30 seconds). The store receives all of them: for order 101 the store got 16 updates and audit saved 7. Per active delivery, Canada actually gets fewer.

Two small cleanups (not urgent)

  • NALA, bloated index: store_brand_order takes 18 MB for 1,756 rows while the table itself is 384 kB. The pkey takes 6.9 MB. Rows are deleted all the time and the index doesn't shrink. REINDEX CONCURRENTLY should bring it under 1 MB.
  • PHUS, unused index: idx_algo_open_orders (42 MB) has never been scanned (idx_scan = 0). PHUS indexes are 5× the size of the table. Consider dropping or rebuilding.

Checked on our side, all clean

  • ALB md-doordash-prod-phus-alb: no 5xx, no rejected connections. Request count fell with the webhooks.
  • daas-doordash: every webhook got a 200 and was forwarded to the gateway in the same second.
  • Gateway → RabbitMQ → store queues: no TTL, no backlog, 0 redeliveries. Broker up 236 h, no alarms.
  • Store apps: each received every update within 1 s of our receipt (zips for 12405, 13669, 30104).
  • No deploy: task version 17 all night. No task restarts (ECS: one task running out of one).
  • CPU/memory (Datadog + CloudWatch): daas-doordash max 16% CPU and 130 MB, gateway max 8% CPU and 223 MB. CPU actually fell during the incident because fewer requests arrived. Engine and audit under 4%.
  • RDS (doordash, gateway, audit): CPU max 2–9%, same as the previous night.
  • ALB: response times the same as the previous night. Request count fell by half (01:00–02:00 UTC) and jumped at 03:00 during the release.
  • Datadog monitors: no CPU, memory, tasks, 5xx or ALB alerts. PHUS “AmazonMQ consumer count” alerts (duplicate consumer on a store queue) fire 15–29 times an hour every night, with no jump in the incident window. The doordash NALA error alerts are “validation error” on rejected cancellations, at the same level as previous nights.
  • DoorDash status page: nothing posted for Oct 14–16.

Side issue on our side (not the cause)

  • daas-doordash sets the status from dasher_status only and forwards backward steps. A late or out-of-order event moves the store backwards: Assigned → Unassigned on driver_batched, and Delivered → Picked up during tonight's release.
  • This happens every night at a similar rate; tonight the delays made it visible. Fix: block status regressions (except cancellation) in internal/providers/doordash/doordash.go.
